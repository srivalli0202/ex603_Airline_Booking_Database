Unit 4 Finding: Actual Booking Failure Rate

Findings for Leadership

After reviewing the booking data, I found that the platform had 200 total bookings. Out pf these, 156 were confirmed, 34 were cancelled, and 10 were disputed. Since cancelled and disputed bookings are considered failures, the actual failure rate is 22%. This means that most bookings were successful, and the failure rate was lower than the dashboard had originally suggested.
The failure rate was different across flights. Some flights had more failed bookings than others. For example, Flight ID 3 had a failure rate of 38.46%, Flight ID 9 had 35.71%, and Flight ID 11 had 31.25%. Each of these flights had five failed bookings.

Based on these results, I recommend investigating Flight IDs 3, 9, and 11 first. These flights had relatively high failure rates and the highest number of failed bookings. The team should review the reasons for cancellations and disputes to understand what caused these problems.
