## Unit 5 Finding: Airline Booking Recommendations
## Recommendations for Leadership
After analyzing the airline booking database, I identified two groups of flights that need attention flights with high booking failure rates and flights with no booking activity.

## Flights with High Failure Rates

Flights 3, 9, 11 had five failed bookings each. Their rates were 38.46%, 35.71%, and 31.25%, respectively. I recommend that leadership investigate these flights first. The team should review cancellation reasons and disputed bookings to understand what caused the failures. Based on the findings, the airline can decide whether changes to booking procedures or customer support are neebed.

Flights 14 and 15 had zero bookings in the dataset. This does not necessarily mean there is a problem. These flights might be new, inactive, or not yet available for booking. I recommend checking their active status and booking availability. If they are active, the team should investigate whether customers can book them and whether customers can book them and whether there is sufficient demand.

The airline should focus on reducing booking failures for flights 3, 9, and 11 while separately investigating why Flights 14 and 15 have no bookings. These actions can help leadership make informed decisions and improve overall booking operations.
